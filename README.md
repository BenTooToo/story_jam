# Story Jam

Most game jams give you a theme. **This one gives you a skeleton.**

Every entry shares the same three fixed moments — an opening, a midpoint, and an
ending — plus the same cast of characters. What happens between those moments is
entirely yours. Why they happen is entirely yours. The anchors lock the images,
never the reasons.

The result: a set of games that hit the exact same three frames and mean
completely different things. That's the whole point. Horror, comedy, romance,
sci-fi — all valid readings of the same skeleton.

## The rules

1. **Hit all three anchors.** They must appear in order. They must be
   recognizable.
2. **Anchors are images, not explanations.** You decide the cause, the motive,
   and the meaning. Nothing about the anchors is canon beyond what's written.
3. **Same cast.** Same names, same relationships, same designs. Everything else
   about them is open.
4. **Fill the gaps freely.** Add any scenes you like between the anchors.
5. **No peeking.** Don't share your story with other entrants until submissions
   close. First reactions are the best part.
6. **Keep it short.** Aim for 10 minutes or less of play. Any engine, any
   format, any art style.

## Running this entry

Built with [Godot 4.7](https://godotengine.org/) (GL Compatibility renderer).

1. Install Godot 4.7 or later.
2. Open the project: launch Godot, click **Import**, and select
   `project.godot` in this folder.
3. Press <kbd>F5</kbd> (or the Play button) to run.
